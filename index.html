Cat Maze Games

Pac-Man style games starring two orange cats. Arrow keys or WASD on a computer, swipe on a phone.

Rhys in PDXEat Churu treats around downtown Portland, dodge the rain clouds, and grab a Baby Doll Pizza slice to drive the RAV4. Magnus in PBEat shrimp across Pacific Beach, San Diego, dodge the vacuum cleaners, and grab a burrito to fight back.
