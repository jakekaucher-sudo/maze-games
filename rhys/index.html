The maze is a simplified downtown Portland. Avenues west to east: 14th, 11th, 10th, Broadway, 6th, 5th, 2nd and Naito Parkway. Streets north to south: Glisan, Everett, Burnside, Stark, Alder, Morrison, Yamhill, Salmon, Madison, Clay and Market. The rain clouds come out of Pioneer Courthouse Square, home of the Weather Machine. Morrison St runs from I-405 across the Morrison Bridge and wraps around. Spot Powell's, Voodoo Doughnut, Big Pink, Lan Su Chinese Garden, Saturday Market, Union Station and Mill Ends Park, the world's smallest park. Extra life at 10,000.
