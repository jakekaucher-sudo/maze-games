The maze is the Pacific Beach street grid. Avenues are named for gems and run in alphabetical order (Diamond, Felspar, Garnet, Hornblend), and the streets east of Mission go Bayard, Cass, Fanuel, Gresham, Ingraham, Lamont. Garnet Ave tunnels from the end of Crystal Pier to the I-5. Kate Sessions Park and Mission Bay are off limits. Extra life at 10,000.
